## Introduction

**Care Alert** is a smart home and healthcare platform designed to bring peace of mind to families by integrating **emergency response**, **mobility monitoring**, and **home automation** into a single mobile application.

> 🌍 This product is delivered through a B2B2C model, reaching users across **Europe**, **Japan**, and **Spain**.

Official Website: [https://www.climax.com.tw/care_alert_platform.php](https://www.climax.com.tw/care_alert_platform.php)

---

## 🔧 Key Features

- 🧠 **Smart Home Automation**  
  Control connected devices through remote access or automation rules.

- 📹 **IP Camera Live Streaming**  
  View your home or loved ones in real time.

- 🚨 **Emergency & Health Sensors**  
  Integrated with emergency buttons, fall detection, and mobility sensors.

- 📖 **Event History**  
  Keep track of key activity logs for safety and diagnostics.

## 🧑‍💻 My Role

While my primary involvement with this project was **maintenance and stability improvements**, I also helped ensure that the mobile experience stayed reliable and secure across new OS versions.

> I assisted in keeping both iOS and Android versions aligned with platform requirements and internal API changes.
