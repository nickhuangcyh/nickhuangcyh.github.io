## Introduction

Asante GarageViewer is the next-generation smart garage door opener that integrates **real-time video streaming**, **voice control**, and **universal compatibility** into one seamless experience.

> 💡 **I independently designed and developed the entire iOS app from scratch.**  
> The app was built using the `VIPER` architecture and implemented with advanced technologies like `TCP/UDP`, `HTTPS`, `p2p`, `RTSP`, `h264`, `FFMpeg`, and both software & hardware decoding.

Through this project, I tackled low-latency live streaming, networked camera integration, and garage door controller compatibility—all while ensuring a clean, scalable codebase and smooth user experience.

## 🔧 Key Features

- 📹 **Real-time Video Streaming**  
  Live view of your garage area from anywhere, anytime, for maximum peace of mind.

- 🗣 **Amazon Alexa Voice Control**  
  Easily open and close your garage using voice commands via Alexa. *(Sensor required)*

- 🌙 **High-Performance Night Vision**  
  Market-leading night vision with wide angle and deep coverage.

- 🚗 **Universal Compatibility**  
  Compatible with almost all garage doors thanks to Asante's smart “magic press” technology.
