## Introduction

**Vesta Home 5** is the official native app for Climax Home Portal — a complete smart home and security control solution deployed in **Europe, Spain, USA and Japan** through a B2B2C model.
Unlike traditional web-based systems, Vesta Home 5 was built natively for iOS and Android to deliver a smooth, real-time user experience for smart home automation.

> 🧠 I led the **core development across both iOS and Android**, including critical features, system integration, and long-term maintenance.  
> 💻 I also introduced and implemented a modern **CI/CD pipeline** using Docker, Jenkins, Fastlane, and Gradle.

Official Website: [https://www.climax.com.tw/home_portal_server.php](https://www.climax.com.tw/home_portal_server.php)

## 🔧 Core Features

- 🛡 **System Arm / Disarm Security**
- 🧠 **Smart Home Automation Control**
- 🔌 **Device & Sensor Integration**
- 🗓 **Event Log & Browsing**
- 📹 **IP Camera Streaming + Snapshot Capture**

## 💡 My Technical Contributions

### 🧩 Core Development (iOS & Android)

- **Communication Protocols**: `TCP/UDP`, `WebSocket`, `HTTPS`, `WebRTC`, `P2P`
- **Native Features**:
  - iOS: `ARKit`, `APNS`, `SwiftyMocky` for unit testing
  - Android: `ARCore Sceneform`, `FCM`, `BluetoothLE`, `mockK` for testing

---

### 🚀 CI/CD Infrastructure

I introduced and maintained a full CI/CD setup for the mobile team, including:

- 🐳 **Docker**: [My Docker Hub](https://hub.docker.com/u/nickhuang9527)
- ⚙️ **Jenkins Pipelines**
- 📦 **Fastlane for iOS**
- ⚙️ **Gradle for Android**

This enabled automated testing, building, and deployment, significantly reducing release time and errors.
