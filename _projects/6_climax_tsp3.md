## Introduction

**TSP-3** is a 7” full-color touchscreen keypad designed for **security system control** and **home automation integration**. It allows users to easily **arm/disarm** their home, view the system status, and operate devices through an intuitive interface — all connected via **Wi-Fi** to the Climax control panel.

> 🛡️ With built-in camera intelligence and privacy controls, TSP-3 balances usability with safety.

Official Product Page:  
[Climax TSP-3 on SourceSecurity](https://www.sourcesecurity.com/climax-technology-tsp-3-audio-video-or-keypad-entry-technical-details.html)

## 🧩 Key Features

- 📱 **7” Touchscreen Display**  
  High-resolution, full-color interface for intuitive control and navigation.

- 🛡️ **Security Panel Control**  
  Supports arm, disarm, and home arm functions via Wi-Fi connectivity with the Climax panel.

- 🏠 **Home Automation Access**  
  Real-time control of sensors and devices directly from the touchscreen.

- 📸 **2MP Front Camera**  
  Captures photos for future algorithm-based analysis (e.g., facial detection, anomaly monitoring).

- 🧘 **Privacy Shutter**  
  Adjustable cover provides camera control and additional peace of mind.

- 🧱 **Flexible Installation**  
  Can be mounted on walls or placed on tables via stand.
