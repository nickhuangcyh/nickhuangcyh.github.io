Asante TapTap 3

Create stunning AI-powered musical light shows with just a tap

Introduction

Asante TapTap 3 is an AI-powered light show creator that transforms music into perfectly synchronized light effects.
Whether you’re setting the mood for a holiday display, party, or outdoor celebration, TapTap 3 lets you effortlessly create a professional-grade musical light show in seconds.

💡 I independently designed and developed the entire iOS app from scratch.
The app was built using MVVM + DataController architecture and involved working with TCP/UDP, HTTPS, and APNS.

This was a solo project where I took full ownership—from architecture, network protocols, and system integration, all the way to publishing the app.
The TapTap 3 app is available on both iOS and Android and pairs with a smart hardware device to deliver immersive light shows.

🔥 Features

  • 🎵 AI-Powered Light Show Generation
    Instantly converts any song into a perfectly synced light show—no editing skills required.

  • 📱 Free & Easy to Use App
    Available on both iOS and Android. Just choose a song, connect to the device, and press play.

  • 💡 All-in-One Gadget
    Combines control system, playback, and storage into one portable unit.

  • 🔊 Dual Mode: Light Show + Bluetooth Speaker
    Supports both pre-programmed light shows and real-time Bluetooth music sync.

  • 🌲 Portable & Outdoor-Ready
    IP54-rated and travel-friendly—perfect for outdoor events, camper vans, and holiday décor.
